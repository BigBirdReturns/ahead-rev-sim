# Code of conduct

## Standard

Participants must communicate with technical precision and basic professional respect. Criticism may be direct and exacting, but it must address claims, mechanisms, evidence, interfaces, or decisions rather than a person's identity or dignity.

Expected conduct includes:

- distinguishing disagreement from misconduct;
- carrying receipts for factual claims;
- stating uncertainty and evidence tier;
- preserving credit and provenance;
- protecting credentials, personal data, confidential information, and restricted material;
- refusing harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, sexualized conduct, impersonation, or deliberate disruption;
- accepting a maintainer's decision to stop an unsafe, unlawful, or unreceipted transaction.

## Technical integrity

This project depends on explicit authority boundaries. Participants must not:

- imply that a third party participated, endorsed, implemented, or accepted work without acknowledged evidence;
- promote modeled or component evidence into a measured complete-system claim;
- hide a denominator, fallback, failure mode, or source restriction;
- upload employer, client, partner, customer, or laboratory material without clear authorization and public licensing;
- use project spaces to solicit credentials, private access, or unauthorized execution.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `jonathan@nodalflow.ai`. Include the relevant repository location, date, participants, and the minimum evidence needed to understand the event. Do not include unnecessary personal or confidential information.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, restrict participation, or refer credible threats and unlawful conduct to the appropriate service or authority. Enforcement decisions should preserve the minimum evidence necessary for accountability while limiting further exposure.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release channels, and project-related private communications when conduct materially affects participation or safety in the project.
