# Commodity Completion Programme

The public compute frontier is treated as a supply field. Companies, universities, standards bodies, open-source communities, national facilities, benchmark organizations, PDKs, foundries, remote systems, and research programmes contribute mechanisms and evidence. They do not receive authority over workload identity, accepted output, software fallback, measurement boundaries, or architectural succession.

The expanded registry contains 73 actors and projects across 25 categories. Every record names official public sources, reusable commodity assets, permitted intake modes, unresolved system gaps, completion questions, and the first transaction that moves the public artifact toward a closed system receipt. The registry is backed by 26 completion gaps and remains constrained to `commodity_only` dependency mode.

## Twelve concurrent completion lanes

The registry is converted into a programme rather than left as a watchlist. The programme covers every record through twelve lanes:

1. `reversible-and-thermodynamic-semantics` turns adiabatic, reversible, stochastic, p-bit, oscillator, and analog mechanisms into workload-bound parity and break-even evidence.
2. `neuromorphic-interoperability` binds immutable model, event, discretization, mapping, reset, output, and host evidence across remote systems, commercial devices, and software fallbacks.
3. `physical-world-and-biological-compute` admits environmental, embodied, reservoir, and molecular dynamics only when held-out task gain exceeds raw input and a pinned digital fallback.
4. `riscv-control-and-hardware-compilers` makes ordinary RISC-V the portable control and provenance plane while CIRCT, Calyx, OpenASIP, Chipyard, and verification stacks remain replaceable implementation machinery.
5. `mixed-signal-and-multiphysics-models` joins circuit, compact-device, electromagnetic, thermal, mechanical, optical, and fluid models under shared time and model-divergence custody.
6. `photonics-and-data-movement` compares useful delivered traffic after laser, SerDes, conversion, package, BER, retry, protocol, cooling, and host costs enter the receipt.
7. `chiplet-and-package-composition` makes UCIe, CXL, OpenHBI, FCSA, chiplet collateral, package models, and roots of trust substitutable behind one state and traffic contract.
8. `open-silicon-and-fabrication` maintains multiple routes from RTL and mixed-signal models to GDS, packaging, test, and measured silicon.
9. `measurement-and-benchmark-custody` binds semantic output, automation, clocks, power, temperature, instruments, uncertainty, and independent acceptance into one reconstructable interval.
10. `security-provenance-and-succession` attests source, firmware, calibration, models, builds, measurements, and supersession without granting the security block workload authority.
11. `intermittent-and-harvested-energy` compares checkpoint, task-atomic, reversible-history, retry, and cartridge execution under identical energy and failure traces.
12. `external-testbeds-and-programme-durability` treats remote systems, national facilities, and funded programmes as replaceable venues whose denial, delay, cancellation, and exit are part of the evidence.

The same commodity may participate in multiple lanes. This is deliberate. A photonic PDK can close design, fabrication, model-fidelity, and measurement seams. A remote neuromorphic system can exercise interoperability, measurement custody, and venue durability. A standard such as UCIe can supply transport semantics while remaining subordinate to the cartridge and accepted-work contract.

## Dispatch

The fan-out engine emits a deterministic SHA-256-sealed completion plan:

```bash
ahead-rev-fanout \
  --priority-max 1 \
  --out artifacts/priority-one-completion-plan.json
```

Individual lanes can be selected without changing registry authority:

```bash
ahead-rev-fanout \
  --lane photonics-and-data-movement \
  --lane chiplet-and-package-composition \
  --priority-max 2 \
  --out artifacts/interconnect-completion-plan.json
```

A plan binds the registry digest, programme digest, lane selection, priority boundary, implicated actors and projects, source locators, commodity locators, first transactions, required receipts, recurring gaps, multi-lane coverage, and plan digest.

## Immediate priority-one floor

Priority one now contains 32 transactions. The additions beyond the initial registry include UCIe and CXL transport profiles, open chiplet collateral, Caliptra attestation, BrainScaleS-2 remote execution, NIR interchange, IBM AIHWKit, THOR testbed preparation, GDSFactory/SAX/gsim photonics, CIRCT and SiliconCompiler lowering, RISC-V differential verification, ngspice/Xyce circuit parity, ReservoirPy baselines, open PDK and LibreLane succession, MLCFlow automation, PTDaemon telemetry, and signed reproducible-build provenance.

The programme does not wait for an actor to finish its own system. It identifies the public asset, wraps it behind our contract, supplies the missing fallback or benchmark, and asks the completion question that the actor's own announcement exposes.

## Claim boundary

Registry inclusion proves only that a public artifact has been identified and mapped to a completion transaction. Programme coverage proves only that every registered commodity has a lane and a required receipt. Neither qualifies performance, energy, volume, timing, thermal closure, fabrication readiness, production maturity, or physical compute. Those claims remain blocked until pinned code, accepted workloads, measured boundaries, and independent reconstruction close them.

The controlling question is: which public claim, codebase, device, standard, testbed, or fabrication route can close a system seam now, and what receipt keeps it replaceable?
