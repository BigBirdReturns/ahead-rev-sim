# Commodity Ecosystem Harvest

The external compute ecosystem is a supply field. Vaire, AheadComputing, Normal Computing, Extropic, RISC-V hosts, neuromorphic systems, photonic fabrics, chiplet standards, benchmark communities, hardware compilers, simulators, PDKs, fabrication services, remote testbeds, national programmes, and biological or environmental computing projects enter as pinned commodities. None receives authority over workload identity, accepted output, software fallback, measurement boundaries, or architecture.

The governing transaction is asymmetric. External actors may contribute code, mechanisms, device models, standards, interfaces, measurements, silicon, test capacity, fabrication access, or failure evidence. They may not redefine accepted work, hide preprocessing or data movement, collapse a multidimensional frontier into a promotional scalar, or promote a component result into a complete-system advantage.

## Machine-readable estate

The authoritative manifest is packaged at:

```text
src/ahead_rev_sim/data/commodity_ecosystem_registry.json
```

It expands 19 independently reviewable shards into one registry containing:

```text
73 actors and projects
25 ecosystem categories
26 completion gaps
32 priority-one transactions
37 priority-two transactions
4 priority-three transactions
```

Every record carries:

- actor, project, category, and public stage;
- official source locators and the completion signal exposed by each source;
- reusable commodity assets, license posture, and allowed intake modes;
- system gaps and completion questions;
- commodity-only policy, immediate actions, priority, and first transaction.

The expanded categories include reversible, thermodynamic, probabilistic, analog, neuromorphic, biological, physical-reservoir, intermittent, photonic, chiplet, RISC-V, hardware-compiler, mixed-signal, multiphysics, benchmark, verification, security, EDA, fabrication, testbed, and programme infrastructure.

The completion taxonomy now covers accepted result, workload identity, host interface, compiler lowering, state and reset, entropy custody, calibration and environment, data movement, complete energy, recovered or harvested energy, timing, thermal state, occupied volume, fallback, independent validation, fabrication, scale-out, model fidelity, measurement reproducibility, governance, task encoding, readout training, precision and conversion, reliability, package completion, security, access, and time synchronization.

## Harvest report

```bash
ahead-rev-commodities \
  --priority-max 1 \
  --out artifacts/commodity-harvest.json
```

The report is deterministic and SHA-256 sealed. It preserves registry identity, selection, source and asset locators, recurring gaps, actions, completion questions, and first transactions.

## Completion programme

The registry is not left as a watchlist. `docs/commodity_completion_program.md` maps all 73 records into twelve concurrent completion lanes. The fan-out engine emits a sealed lane and actor plan:

```bash
ahead-rev-fanout \
  --priority-max 1 \
  --out artifacts/priority-one-completion-plan.json
```

This arrangement makes every announcement actionable. A tape-out becomes a characterization target. A simulator becomes a mandatory fallback. A standard becomes a transport profile. A PDK becomes one implementation route. A remote system becomes a qualification venue. A cancelled programme becomes a succession and exit test. Every contribution competes behind the same accepted-work and receipt authority.

## Evidence boundary

The registry records public claims and artifacts as intake leads. Inclusion does not qualify the originating actor's physical, performance, energy, volume, timing, thermal, fabrication, reliability, access, or production claims. Those claims remain blocked until the corresponding code, workload, measurement, silicon, and independent acceptance artifacts are consumed through the relevant receipt.

The control question for every press release, repository, standard, paper, demo, facility, or tape-out is: what can be pinned and consumed immediately, which system seam remains open, and what receipt makes the contribution replaceable?
